Meet me at the old lighthouse on the north pier at midnight on Friday. Bring the brass key
from the drawer under the stairs, a torch with fresh batteries and a warm coat, because the
wind off the water is brutal this time of year. Do not tell anyone where you are going, not
even your sister. If the gate is locked, follow the fence to the left until you reach the
broken plank, then squeeze through and wait by the second bench. I will flash the torch
three times when it is safe to come up. The map we found in the attic shows a room under
the lamp that nobody has opened in forty years. Whatever is inside, we split it evenly,
and we never speak of it again.
